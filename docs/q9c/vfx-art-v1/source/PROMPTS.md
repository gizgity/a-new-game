Unlike the enemy's thin red circle and unlike player Charged's amber explosion.

Outer source brackets and 3-4 inward jagged cracks that grow INSIDE the actual circular boundary, leave center and player legible. Distinct from storm via ground fissures not bolt; distinct from Volatile enemy explosion via bracketed origin and plate silhouette.
4 STATIC FIELD: actual Perfect Dodge counter to up to four real nearby targets. Brief angular thin lavender/ivory zigzag spokes from player's edge to those targets, with clipped bright corner sparks, not a full-radius filled field. At enemy extra-projectile drawback, show two short FORKED shard projectiles with lavender tails versus the unchanged ordinary round Archer projectile; same actual hitbox/speed, not more bullets than the mechanic. No new lightning Sigil mark, no borrowed Rupture pips.
5 BROKEN SEAL: actual Heavy on a common enemy creates a compact ivory fractured-seal impact, two pieces split outward along force. During existing brief stagger-resistance window show two detached neutral notched brackets with a short diagonal broken slash at FOOT level. No enclosing cyan barrier, no shield, no fake armor/HP, no Boss/Elite CC. Actor always visible.
6 ECHO CHAMBER: one extra relay beyond native detonate range to an already marked enemy. Use a thin offset lavender broken echo trail plus a paired clipped corner glyph at the extra relay landing, while preserving baseline mint Rupture marks. Retaliation uses the COMMON bracketed circular warning radius50, 1.2s, with two short nested arc echoes inside it and a tiny source notch. A thin source-to-zone trace is informational, not a damaging beam. No new damage footprint or repeated extra targets.
No fog, no broad glow/bloom, no noisy particles, no neon floor repaint, no photorealism, no smooth 3D, no cinematic perspective, no redesigned actors/HUD. Avoid large opaque shapes. All effect sizes relative to player body height40 and actual world radii; do not enlarge effects simply to sell the concept. Put the board heading 'ANOMALY VFX — CONCEPT V1' and a small note 'PROPOSED · NO GAMEPLAY CHANGES' outside the cells. Show a professional game-art vocabulary study, not an infographic packed with prose.
